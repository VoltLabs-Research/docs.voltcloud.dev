title: Chill+ Ice & Hydrate Classifier
description: Classify water molecules as hexagonal ice, cubic ice, interfacial ice, clathrate hydrate, interfacial hydrate or liquid with the CHILL+ algorithm.
---

CHILL+ tells ice and clathrate hydrates apart from liquid water using the correlation of local bond-orientational order between neighboring molecules. Use it to follow ice nucleation and growth, stacking disorder (cubic versus hexagonal ice) and hydrate formation in water simulations.

| Key | Version | Builds |
|---|---|---|
| `chill-plus` | 1.1.1 | Linux, macOS, Windows, browser |

## How it works

Every particle in the frame is treated as a water molecule. For each one, the plugin computes the complex bond-order vector q₃ₘ (m = −3…3) from the spherical harmonics of the bonds to the neighbors within the cutoff. For each bond it then takes the normalized correlation c(i,j) of the two molecules' q₃ vectors:

- **Staggered** bond: c(i,j) < −0.8
- **Eclipsed** bond: −0.35 < c(i,j) < 0.25

A molecule with exactly four neighbors is classified from its bond counts:

| Bonds | Structure |
|---|---|
| 4 eclipsed | `HYDRATE` |
| 3 eclipsed | `INTERFACIAL_HYDRATE` |
| 4 staggered | `CUBIC_ICE` |
| 3 staggered and 1 eclipsed | `HEXAGONAL_ICE` |
| 3 staggered and no eclipsed, or 2 staggered | `INTERFACIAL_ICE` |

Everything else, including any molecule without exactly four neighbors, is `OTHER` (liquid).

## Inputs

- Positions of one particle per water molecule: the oxygen atoms, or the sites of a coarse-grained model such as mW. The plugin doesn't filter by type, so a frame that also holds hydrogens or guest molecules gives wrong neighbor counts.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Default | Allowed values | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O-O Cutoff (Å)** `cutoff` | number | `3.5` | 2 to 10, step 0.1 | Neighbor cutoff between water molecules. 3.5 Å sits in the first minimum of the O–O radial distribution function of water. |

## Outputs

| Exposure | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Chill+ Structure** | A listing, and a 3D model of the molecules colored by structure. |
| **Chill+ Summary** | The frame's totals: atoms, the cutoff, and molecules per structure. Stored with the analysis. No listing. |

The workflow declares one per-atom property, `structure_type` (Structure Type), with the values `0` OTHER, `1` HEXAGONAL_ICE, `2` CUBIC_ICE, `3` INTERFACIAL_ICE, `4` HYDRATE, `5` INTERFACIAL_HYDRATE.
