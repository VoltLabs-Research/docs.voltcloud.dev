---
title: Jobs
description: Follow the work clusters do for your team, frame by frame, and remove or retry it.
---

Everything a cluster does for your team runs as jobs: processing an uploaded trajectory, rendering its preview,
computing an analysis, uploading results. Each job covers one piece of work, usually one frame. The jobs view
lets you follow them as they run, see which frames failed and why, and stop or retry them.

Jobs are team-wide: every member sees the jobs of every trajectory in the selected team, whoever started them.
The list updates live.

## Where to find them

- **Compute jobs** in the [dashboard header](/docs/guides/dashboard#the-header): the ring fills as jobs complete,
  and the label reads **Jobs**, `2 running` or `5 queued`. Click it to open the popover.
- **Compute jobs** in a trajectory card's menu opens a panel at the right of the dashboard with that trajectory's
  jobs only.

The popover shows:

- The counts: **Queued**, **Running**, **Done** and **Failed**.
- The jobs of all trajectories, grouped as described below.
- **Clusters**: the team's clusters' average **CPU**, **Memory**, **Disk** and **Network**, from those reporting
  live metrics. CPU turns red at 85 %.
- `3 / 5 online`, the team members online, linking to **My Team**.
- **Open clusters**, to the [clusters](/docs/guides/clusters) page.

When VOLT runs in your browser tab or in the desktop app, **Clusters** and **Open clusters** don't appear. On
single-user deployments the online count doesn't appear.

## How jobs are grouped

Jobs are listed in three levels, most recent trajectory first:

| Level | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Trajectory | Its name, `12/20 jobs` completed and when the last job changed. Click to expand. |
| Frame | **Frame** followed by the timestep, **General** for jobs not tied to a frame, or **Storage Transfer** / **Storage Transfers** |
| Job | Its name, how long ago it changed, how long it took once completed, and its error if it failed |

Groups that contain storage transfers open by themselves.

The color of a name tells its state: amber while queued, blue while running, green once completed, red if it
failed. A trajectory or frame whose jobs are partly done and partly failed keeps a neutral color. A trajectory
that has just finished all its jobs briefly glows green.

## Job types

| Job | Started by | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Preprocess trajectory frame** | An upload | Stores the frame and builds its 3D model. See [Processing](/docs/guides/trajectories#processing). |
| **Rasterize trajectory preview** | An upload | Renders the preview image of the trajectory card |
| The plugin's name, such as **Polyhedral Template Matching** | A pipeline run | Computes that analysis on that frame |
| **Artifact Upload** | An analysis | Stores an analysis's 3D results |
| **Trajectory Transfer**, **Analysis Transfer**, **Storage Transfer** | The clusters | Moves stored data from one cluster to another |

A pipeline run with several analyses shows one job per analysis under each frame: the cluster runs all the
stages of a frame in one go, in order.

## Statuses

| Status | Counted as | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `queued` | **Queued** | Waiting in the cluster's queue for a free worker |
| `retrying` | **Queued** | Queued again after a retry |
| `running` | **Running** | A worker is on it |
| `completed` | **Done** | Finished |
| `failed` | **Failed** | Failed for good. The error is shown under the job's name. |

Jobs run on the cluster that holds the work: trajectory processing on the cluster that stores the trajectory,
analyses on the cluster the pipeline ran on. A job waits in **Queued** until a worker of that cluster picks it
up, so a busy cluster shows long queues while an idle one starts jobs at once. A trajectory frame job gets up
to three attempts before it is marked failed.

A job stays in the list for 24 hours after its last change, then drops out.

## Actions

Right-click a trajectory's group for:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove Running Jobs** | Stops the trajectory's queued, running and retrying jobs on their clusters and removes them from the list |
| **Retry Failed Jobs** | Queues every failed job of the trajectory again |

Toasts report the outcome: `Removed 4 job(s)`, `Queued 2 failed job(s) for retry`, or
**No queued or running jobs found** / **No failed jobs found to retry**. If a cluster doesn't confirm, the toast
says so.

A failed analysis job also has its own **Retry** button, which retries the failed frames of that analysis. The
[analysis listing](/docs/guides/pipelines#the-analysis-listing) offers the same as **Retry Failed Frames**.

Removing jobs doesn't delete what they already produced. To delete results, delete the analysis; see
[Deleting results](/docs/guides/results#deleting-results).

## Completion notices

While a trajectory's jobs panel is open, a toast appears when its work finishes: **Analysis completed
successfully!**, **Rasterization completed successfully**, or **Jobs completed successfully**, and the same with
"with errors" when some jobs failed.
