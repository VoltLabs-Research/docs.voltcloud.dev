The dashboard at `/dashboard` is where you land after signing in. It shows the selected team's trajectories
and folders as cards, and it is wrapped in the chrome every dashboard page shares: the sidebar on the left,
the header on top, and, on narrow screens, a tab bar at the bottom.

<Screenshot name="dashboard" alt="The dashboard: the sidebar, the header with the team selector and search, and the grid of folders and trajectories" />

Everything you see belongs to the team selected in the header. Switch teams and the page, the search and
the jobs list follow.

## The page

The page is titled **Trajectories**. Members who can create trajectories get two buttons next to the title:

- **New folder** opens **New Trajectory Folder** and creates a folder in the folder you are in.
- **Upload** opens your system's file picker. See [Uploading](/docs/guides/trajectories#uploading).

You can also drop files or folders anywhere on the page to upload them. While you drag, the page reads
**Drop files to upload them.**

Below the title is a grid with the folders of the current location first, then its trajectories. Inside a
folder, breadcrumbs above the grid lead back to its parents and to **Root**.

### Trajectory cards

A trajectory card shows the trajectory's preview image, its name and a caption:

- While the trajectory is processing, the caption is its stage (**Queued...**, **Processing frames...**).
- Once it is ready, the caption reads `Edited 3 minutes ago · 120,000 atoms · 50 frames`.

Click a ready card, or focus it and press Enter, to open it in the [canvas](/docs/guides/canvas). Cards of
trajectories that are still processing or that failed do not open. Double-click the name to rename the
trajectory.

The **⋮** button in the card's corner opens its actions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **View scene** | Opens the trajectory in the canvas. Disabled until processing completes. |
| **Compute jobs** | Opens a panel at the right with this trajectory's jobs. See [Jobs](/docs/guides/jobs). |
| **Export** | Downloads every frame as a ZIP. See [Downloads](/docs/guides/trajectories#downloads). |
| **Move to folder** | Opens **Move Trajectory** to pick a destination folder. |
| **Delete** | Asks for confirmation, then deletes the trajectory and everything computed from it. |

Right-click a card for the same actions as the trajectory listing: **View Scene**, **Inspect Atoms** (the
[per-atom viewer](/docs/guides/trajectories#per-atom-viewer) at the first frame), **Export**, **Move to Folder**,
**Delete trajectory** and **Copy Document ID**.

To move a trajectory without the dialog, drag its card onto a folder card or onto a breadcrumb.

### Folder cards

A folder card fans out the previews of up to three of the most recently edited trajectories in that folder
and its subfolders. An empty folder reads **No trajectories yet**. The caption under the card shows when the
folder was last edited.

Click a folder card to open it. Its **…** button offers **Open Folder**, **Rename Folder** and **Delete Folder**;
right-clicking the card adds **Copy Document ID**. Deleting a folder deletes its subfolders and every trajectory
inside them.

### Empty states

| Situation | What the page shows |
| --- | --- |
| You don't belong to any team yet | **Create your first team**, and the team creation dialog opens |
| The team has no trajectories | **No simulations yet** with **Download sample simulations** |
| After downloading the samples | **Sample simulations ready** with **Upload trajectory** |
| The folder you opened is empty | **No trajectories in this folder** with **Upload trajectory** |

**Download sample simulations** saves the server's sample files to your computer. Upload them like any other
file to get a first trajectory to work with. See [Sample simulations](/docs/guides/trajectories#sample-simulations).

## The header

From left to right:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Collapse sidebar** / **Expand sidebar** | Narrows the sidebar to icons or widens it back. The choice is remembered in this browser. |
| Team switcher | Lists your teams. Pick one to switch. Hover a team to show its **Leave team** button. |
| **Team actions** (**+**) | **Create team** and **Join existing team**. |
| Global search | Searches the selected team. See below. |
| Compute jobs | A ring and a label (**Jobs**, `2 running`, `5 queued`) that opens the jobs popover. See [Jobs](/docs/guides/jobs). |
| **Invite members** | Opens the invitation panel. Disabled unless you can invite. See [Teams](/docs/guides/teams). |
| Theme | Switches between light and dark. |
| VOLT AI | Opens the floating assistant. See [VOLT AI](/docs/guides/volt-ai). |
| Notifications | See [Notifications](/docs/guides/notifications). |

On single-user deployments the team switcher, **Team actions** and **Invite members** are hidden.

### Global search

Type at least two characters in **Search…**, or press Ctrl K (⌘ K on macOS) to jump to the field. Results
appear after a short pause, grouped in up to four sections of five results each:

| Section | Matches | Selecting a result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Analyses** | Plugin name, the name of the trajectory it ran on, or an exact analysis ID | Opens the trajectory in the canvas |
| **Trajectories** | Trajectory name | Opens it in the canvas. Trajectories that haven't finished processing are grayed out. |
| **Plugins** | Plugin name or description | Opens the plugin's first results listing, or **Installed** if it has none |
| **Teams** | Name or description of the teams you belong to | Switches to that team and opens **My Team** |

Use the arrow keys to move through the results and Enter to open one. Escape closes the list.

On phones the search is a button in the header that opens the field over it.

## The sidebar

The sidebar holds the main navigation:

| Entry | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | `/dashboard` |
| **Notebooks** | `/dashboard/notebooks`. See [Notebooks](/docs/guides/notebooks). |
| **Trajectories** | `/dashboard/trajectories/list`, and switches the sidebar to the trajectories section |
| **Analysis** | Expands to **View all** (`/dashboard/analysis-configs/list`) and one entry per plugin that has results listings, each with its listings. See [Results](/docs/guides/results). |
| **Plugins** | Expands to **Installed** and **Marketplace**. See [Plugins](/docs/guides/plugins). |
| **Clusters** | Expands to **View all** and one entry per cluster with **Monitor**. See [Clusters](/docs/guides/clusters). |
| **My Team**, **Manage Roles** | Team pages. See [Teams](/docs/guides/teams). |
| **VOLT AI** | `/dashboard/ai` |
| **Whiteboards** | `/dashboard/whiteboards`. See [Whiteboards](/docs/guides/whiteboards). |
| **Settings** | Your account settings. See [Settings](/docs/guides/settings). |
| **Read the docs** | This site, in a new tab |

Your account menu sits at the bottom of the sidebar.

Entries you lack the permission for stay visible but disabled, with a tooltip that says why. **My Team** and
**Manage Roles** don't appear on single-user deployments. **Clusters** doesn't appear when VOLT runs in your
browser tab or in the desktop app, where the app itself is the only cluster.

### Sections

Some areas take over the sidebar instead of opening a second column. They start with **Go back**:

- **Trajectories** lists **Trajectories**, **Artifacts** and **Simulation cells**. While the per-atom viewer
  is open it also lists **Per-atom properties**. See [Trajectories](/docs/guides/trajectories).
- **Settings** lists the account settings pages.
- **Secret keys** lists **Keys** and **Metrics**.
- **VOLT AI** lists your conversations.

## Phones and narrow windows

Below 1024 px wide the sidebar becomes a drawer. Open it with **Open navigation** in the header.

A tab bar at the bottom gives one-tap access to **Dashboard**, **Trajectories**, **Upload trajectory**, **VOLT AI**
and **Settings**. **Upload trajectory** uploads into the folder you are in when you are on the dashboard or the
trajectory listing, and into the root folder elsewhere.
