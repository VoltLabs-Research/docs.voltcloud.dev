---
title: Simulation cells
description: The simulation box VOLT records for every frame, and the listing that shows them across a team.
---

A simulation cell is the box a frame was simulated in: its three edge vectors, its origin and which axes are
periodic. VOLT records one per frame when a trajectory is uploaded, read from the frame's header. The canvas
uses it to draw the box and its periodic images.

## What a record holds

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Trajectory | The trajectory the frame belongs to |
| Timestep | The frame's timestep |
| Cell vectors | The edge vectors **a**, **b** and **c**, in ångströms |
| Cell origin | The corner the vectors start from |
| Periodic boundary conditions | Whether X, Y and Z are periodic |
| Bounding box | **Width** (the x component of **a**), **Length** (the y component of **b**) and **Height** (the z component of **c**) |

For an orthogonal box the bounding box is the box's size. For a triclinic cell it holds only the diagonal
components; the full shape is in the cell vectors.

Records are created when the upload is committed, before the frames are processed, and they are deleted with
their trajectory. You can't edit them.

## The listing

**Simulation cells** in the trajectories section of the sidebar opens `/dashboard/simulation-cells/list`, the
cells of every frame of every trajectory in the team.

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Trajectory** | The trajectory's name. Click it for the trajectory's details. |
| **Timestep** | The frame |
| **Width (Å)**, **Height (Å)**, **Length (Å)** | The bounding box, to three decimals |
| **Boundary Conditions** | **Periodic · X Y Z** for a fully periodic box, otherwise one chip per axis, such as **Z: Open** |
| **Created At** | When the record was made |

Sort by any column except **Boundary Conditions** and **Created At** by clicking its header. Right-click a row
for **Copy Document ID**.

## In the canvas

The canvas shows the cell of the frame on screen in two places:

- **Simulation cell** under **Visual elements** in the pipeline panel draws the box. Its checkbox shows or hides
  it, and selecting it lets you set its opacity. See [Canvas](/docs/guides/canvas#visual-elements).
- The **Simulation Cell** tab of the timeline lists the **Bounding Box**, **Boundary Conditions**,
  **Cell Vectors** and **Cell Origin** of the current frame.

The **Simulation Cell** tab also has **Show PBC images**, which outlines the neighboring copies of the box
along each periodic axis, and **Edit Cell**, which lets you change the a/b/c vectors and the periodic axes and **Apply** them. The edit only
changes how the canvas draws the cell in your browser; the stored record stays as it is. **Reset Cell** returns to
the stored cell.
